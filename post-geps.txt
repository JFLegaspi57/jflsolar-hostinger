## The big launch: PowerStack 3.0
The highlight was the launch of PowerStack 3.0, Sungrow's next-generation energy storage system for commercial and industrial (C&I) sites. Sungrow calls it the move from "All-in-One" to "Scene-in-One": instead of one box for every job, the system is built around the job it has to do.
A "multi-scenario dock" with four modules (PCS, Solar, Charging and Microgrid) lets the same platform work as:
- Standalone storage: power modules can be combined for 2-hour or 4-hour systems.
- Solar plus storage: DC-coupled, without an extra inverter.
- Solar, storage and EV charging: all three DC-coupled in one system.
- Microgrid: critical loads stay powered during an outage, with a 0 ms switchover and no separate static transfer switch (STS) cabinet.
## The numbers that caught my eye
- Compact: up to 256 kWh per square metre, up to 20 units in parallel, and a 43% smaller footprint for a 5 MWh plant compared with the previous generation.
- Efficient: a silicon carbide (SiC) power converter with up to 99.2% conversion efficiency, and over 92% round-trip efficiency with 684 Ah stacked cells.
- Tough: IP55 protection, C5-level corrosion protection, operation from -30°C to 50°C, and tolerance of up to 30% grid harmonics.
- Safe: three-level fuse protection and a four-layer fire safety design.
A few of these deserve a plain-language explanation. Round-trip efficiency tells you how much energy comes back out of the battery: of every 100 kWh stored, more than 92 kWh can be used again. C5 corrosion protection is the highest class for harsh environments, which matters for sites near the sea, and we have a lot of those in the Philippines. Tolerance for grid harmonics matters in factories, where motors, pumps and drives make the grid "noisy" and can trip less robust equipment.
## AI that works alongside the installer
PowerStack 3.0 comes with Bubbly, Sungrow's own AI assistant, which supports the whole life of a project:
- Design: satellite imagery and 3D modelling produce a design proposal, with layout and financial calculation, in about 30 seconds.
- Commissioning: automatic configuration and data checks in about 3 minutes.
- Operation: smart scheduling across self-consumption, demand control, energy arbitrage and frequency regulation. Sungrow says this can bring returns more than 12% higher than plain self-consumption.
- Maintenance: 24/7 monitoring that watches for more than 30 potential risks, and a fault report for a 10 MWh plant in about a minute.
Having designed and managed C&I solar PV installations for years, I can tell you that design and commissioning are where projects lose the most time. Tools like this don't replace a good engineer, but they let a good engineer do more, faster and with fewer mistakes.
## What this means for Filipino businesses
Electricity is one of the biggest costs for many Philippine businesses, and many are billed not only for the energy they use but also for their highest power demand. Solar plus storage designed around the site, to cut those peaks, store midday solar for later and keep critical loads running during brownouts, is exactly where C&I energy is heading. PowerStack 3.0 shows that direction very clearly.
